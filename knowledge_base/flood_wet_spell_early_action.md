# Heavy Rainfall, Flood, and Wet Spell Early Action Guidance

## Community actions
- Avoid flood-prone river crossings and low-lying routes during heavy rainfall.
- Clear local drainage channels and protect household assets.
- Move livestock away from flood-prone grazing areas where possible.

## Policymaker and DRM actions
- Monitor flood-prone roads, bridges, drainage points, and settlements.
- Prepare warning messages for heavy rainfall and access disruption.
- Coordinate with water, health, transport, and disaster risk management offices.

## Humanitarian priorities
- Prepare support for temporary displacement and road access disruption.
- Monitor disease risks related to standing water and poor sanitation.
