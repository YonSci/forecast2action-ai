# Rainfed Agriculture, Agro-pastoral, and Livestock Advisory

## Rainfed agriculture
- Match planting decisions to rainfall onset and short-term forecast confidence.
- Promote drought-tolerant or short-cycle varieties under dry-spell risk.
- Encourage soil moisture conservation, mulching, and small-scale water harvesting.

## Agro-pastoral communities
- Monitor pasture regeneration and water-point access.
- Coordinate local grazing information to reduce conflict and movement stress.

## Livestock
- Track body condition, milk production, unusual mortality, and disease signals.
- Prepare animal health services and targeted feed support in high-risk areas.
