# SMS and WhatsApp Message Guidance

## SMS
- Keep messages short, clear, and action-oriented.
- Mention the area, hazard, lead time, risk level, and one or two priority actions.
- Use local languages where possible.

## WhatsApp
- Include area, forecast lead, hazard, risk level, and recommended actions.
- Use bullet points for readability.
- Clearly distinguish forecast information from confirmed impacts.
