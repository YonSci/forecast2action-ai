# Drought Early Action Guidance

## Farmer and agro-pastoral actions
- Conserve available water and strengthen household or farm-level water storage.
- Use drought-tolerant crop varieties where feasible.
- Delay planting where rainfall onset is uncertain or dry-spell risk is high.
- Monitor pasture condition, livestock body condition, and water-point access.

## Policymaker and DRM actions
- Verify water-point functionality in high-risk woredas.
- Coordinate agriculture, livestock, water, health, and disaster risk management offices.
- Prioritize advisories through extension workers, SMS, WhatsApp, and local radio.

## Humanitarian priorities
- Pre-position water support and water-point maintenance capacity.
- Prioritize livestock feed, animal health, and veterinary support in pastoral areas.
