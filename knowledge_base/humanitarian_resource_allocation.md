# Humanitarian Resource Allocation Guidance

## Prioritization principles
- Prioritize areas where hazard probability, exposure, and vulnerability are jointly high.
- Give special attention to woredas with high risk scores and confirmed community impacts.
- Use local verification to distinguish forecast risk from observed impact.

## Resource allocation examples
- Water support: water-point rehabilitation, water trucking contingency, spare parts.
- Livestock support: feed, animal health campaigns, veterinary supplies.
- Agriculture support: drought-tolerant seed, extension messaging, pest monitoring.
