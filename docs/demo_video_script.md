# Forecast2Action AI — Demo Video Script

## Recommended Video Length

**2–3 minutes**

Recommended screen layout:

- Browser showing the Forecast2Action AI dashboard;
- Terminal or VS Code only if briefly needed;
- Keep the demo focused on the user workflow, not code.

---

## Demo Goal

Show that Forecast2Action AI moves from climate risk evidence to operational early action.

Core message:

```text
Forecast2Action AI transforms climate risk signals into explainable advisories, priority actions, community messages, implementation tasks, and exportable bulletins.
```

---

## 1. Opening — 10 seconds

### What to show

Show the dashboard homepage with the title **Forecast2Action AI**.

### Voice-over

“Forecast2Action AI is an early warning copilot designed to help climate and disaster-risk teams move from forecasts to practical early action. Many systems show maps or rainfall outlooks, but decision-makers still need to know what action to take, who should act, and how to communicate clearly with communities.”

---

## 2. Problem Statement — 15 seconds

### What to show

Keep the dashboard visible. Slowly scroll to show the risk cards and map.

### Voice-over

“In the IGAD region, communities face drought, floods, heat stress, livestock losses, crop impacts, and water shortages. Forecast information is valuable, but there is often a gap between early warning and early action. Forecast2Action AI closes this gap by converting climate risk signals into operational decisions.”

---

## 3. Dashboard Overview — 15 seconds

### What to show

Show the top dashboard metrics:

- Countries;
- Hazards;
- Warnings;
- Triggers.

Then show the risk map.

### Voice-over

“The dashboard summarizes monitored countries, hazards, warning areas, and trigger areas. It also provides an interactive risk map showing where climate-related risks are emerging.”

## 4. Select a District — 15 seconds

### What to show

Select **Borena, Ethiopia** from the district dropdown.

### Voice-over

“For this demo, I select Borena in Ethiopia. The system updates the map, advisory, community reports, and implementation tracker for the selected district.”

## 5. Climate Evidence — 20 seconds

### What to show

Scroll to the **Forecast-to-Action Advisory** panel and show:

- Seasonal rainfall;
- Baseline mean;
- Rainfall anomaly;
- Anomaly percent;
- SPI-like score.

### Voice-over

“The system starts with district-level rainfall evidence. It compares seasonal rainfall against a baseline and calculates rainfall anomaly and an SPI-like score. These indicators help classify the hazard and estimate the level of climate stress.”

## 6. Impact-Based Risk Score — 15 seconds

### What to show

Show the impact-risk inputs:

- Hazard probability;
- Exposure;
- Vulnerability;
- Confidence;
- Risk level.

### Voice-over

“Forecast2Action AI combines hazard probability, exposure, vulnerability, and forecast confidence into an impact-based risk score. The score is classified into no alert, watch, warning, or trigger.”

## 7. Priority Action Queue — 20 seconds

### What to show

Point to:

- Risk score;
- Priority score;
- Community reports;
- Rainfall anomaly;
- SPI-like score.

### Voice-over

“The priority action queue ranks districts based on risk score and community feedback. This helps disaster risk managers and partners quickly identify where early action should be prioritized first.”

## 8. Local RAG-Style Advisory — 25 seconds

### What to show

Scroll to:

- Recommended early actions;
- Role-specific advisory;
- Knowledge-guided advisory basis;
- Retrieved knowledge sources.

“The AI component is a local RAG-style advisory engine. Instead of generating unsupported generic text, it retrieves relevant early action guidance from a structured Action Knowledge Base. Retrieval is based on hazard type, risk level, target audience, rainfall anomaly, SPI-like score, and community feedback. This makes the advisory explainable and auditable.”

# Short 2-Minute Version

Use this version if the video must be very short.

## Voice-over

“Forecast2Action AI is an early warning copilot that helps decision-makers move from climate forecasts to practical early action.

The system starts with district-level rainfall evidence, including rainfall anomaly and an SPI-like score. It combines this information with exposure, vulnerability, and forecast confidence to generate an impact-based risk score.

The dashboard shows monitored countries, hazards, warning areas, trigger areas, and an interactive risk map. The priority action queue ranks districts so decision-makers can quickly identify where early action should be prioritized.

For each district, Forecast2Action AI generates a knowledge-guided advisory. The AI component uses a local RAG-style engine that retrieves relevant early action guidance from a structured Action Knowledge Base. This makes the advisory transparent, explainable, and grounded in local decision-support knowledge.

The system also supports different audiences, including disaster risk managers, NGO planners, extension officers, and community members. It generates SMS and WhatsApp-ready community messages in English, Amharic, and Swahili.

Community reports can be submitted to ground-truth the forecast signal. These include observations such as water shortage, pasture stress, crop wilting, livestock stress, or flooded roads.

Recommended actions are converted into an Action Implementation Tracker with responsible sectors, priorities, deadlines, and saved task status. The tracker can be exported as CSV.

Finally, the system generates an operational early warning bulletin in HTML or Markdown, including climate evidence, risk explanation, recommended actions, action tracking, and community messages.

Forecast2Action AI helps close the gap between early warning and early action, supporting smarter decisions and stronger communities across the IGAD region.”

- The dashboard loads without errors;
- Risk map is visible;

- Action Implementation Tracker is visible;
